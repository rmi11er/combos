Twelve of 16 observed dates are positive. Every one of 428 native-fixture omissions leaves positive net, ranging from $6,778,353.03 to $13,833,153.62. These overlapping omissions are influence checks, not independent confidence intervals. The curve still depends heavily on late-July gains; the different leg/structure segments have materially different paths. A strong endpoint does not imply stable expected edge on each incoming RFQ.

Through July 25, aggregate net was −$203,614.16: distinct-fixture combos lost $4,059,888.64 while same-game structures earned $3,856,274.48. The final three observed dates account for the reversal. This date concentration is material even though every single-fixture omission is positive.

The narrower priced cohort covers 467,583 prints across 15 dates and only $332,448.53 sell-all net. It is restricted to matched-source, 2–4-leg, distinct-fixture, pregame core markets. Broad-universe gains cannot be assigned to that opportunity set, nor to its model-selected or funded portfolios. Compare denominators before judging selection quality. The continuous 48-policy replay remains a separate pending artifact.

Float report totals reconcile to independent Decimal totals within $0.003 across the multi-billion-dollar totals; P&L differs by less than $0.000005. Native quantities, prices, fees and scalar/binary settlements were audited per day. Fee effective dates remain unverified scenarios; this accounting verification does not certify applicability. No future settlement P&L is made spendable at trade time.
