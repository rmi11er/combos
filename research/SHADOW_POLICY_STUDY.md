# Broad quoting and source disagreement: reusable shadow study

The opt-in `shadow_policy_study` runtime configuration evaluates four candidate policies for every successfully priced, fixed-contract RFQ: the existing stressed model, the existing three-source point fair, Kalshi-only point fair, and Polymarket-only point fair. Each produces a cash-fee-aware minimum seller YES ask and additional commercial markups of 0, 0.5, 1 and 2 cents per contract above that floor. All use the same configured minimum net profit, native NO price grid and exact single-full-fill cash-fee engine.

The study runs after the baseline decision, including when the baseline has no feasible stressed price or fails a risk limit. This corrects a limitation in the older `ShadowQuoteAudit`: that sidecar ran only after the baseline had already found a price. A public print is not required for candidate evaluation. A rejected historical execution price does not imply no valid quote exists at a different price.

Every decision gets a study status or explicit unavailable/blocked reason. Creator, duplicate, processing lag, scope, pregame, reviewed payoff mapping, source freshness, actual forward depth and disagreement-halt gates remain binding. The study cannot restore missing observations or convert imputed historical depth into live depth. Candidate risk checks are explicitly not performed; the ledger includes baseline risk results and pre-decision capital availability, and each candidate reports its own maximum loss. No candidate creates a reservation or replaces a baseline quote.

Point variants assume independent distinct fixtures and explicitly zero, unestimated uncertainty/dependence/adverse-selection/inventory reserves. Existing strategy-signal edge remains present. These are diagnostic alternatives, not approved executable prices. They are labeled `deployable: false`; acceptance, selected candidate and realized candidate P&L remain absent. Current model bounds are also labeled uncalibrated. Neither a price crossing on public tape nor a shadow candidate is an acceptance label.

The study records selected-side and canonical-YES fee-expanded intervals, actual two-sided depth, fee provenance/context, source and availability timestamps, freshness, source references, mapping references and current blend weights for Kalshi and Polymarket. It reports each leg's signed PM-minus-Kalshi probability difference, nonoverlapping interval gap, and independent combo disagreement. A selected NO leg reverses the sign of the canonical-YES disagreement. Existing decision pricing retains all three-source context, including Pinnacle. Greater PM sharpness remains an unvalidated, sport-specific hypothesis.

`config/shadow_policy_study.json` contains separate `mlb-shadow-candidates-v1` and `nfl-shadow-candidates-v1` profiles. Their initial variants/markups match deliberately; neither sport inherits empirical calibration from the other. The complete profile configuration and code hashes enter the runtime identity. The template has null fee/policy availability times and cash precision: a preparer must supply verified metadata instead of silently assuming deployment facts.

Prepare a **new** runtime experiment with `scripts/configure_shadow_policy_study.py --runtime <reviewed-runtime.json> --out <new-runtime.json> --balance-precision <verified-precision> --fee-terms-available-at <verified-UTC-Unix-time>`. Policy availability defaults to preparation time; a newly chosen policy must not be backdated to make an old replay appear prospective. Then use the ordinary `scripts/runtime.py` initialization/run/archive lifecycle with the new runtime file. Existing experiments remain unchanged. MLB still requires a reviewed MLB runtime/mapping set; the study profile alone does not provide one.

After archiving, `scripts/shadow_policy_report.py --archive <archive-directory> --out <new-report.json>` verifies chunk hashes and counts, then summarizes baseline gates, study coverage, feasible candidate floors, disagreement directions and variant counts. It does not sum alternative quotes as volume or fills, and labels old uninstrumented decisions explicitly. Compare elapsed decision latency on a new bounded capture before increasing collection duration; extra candidate pricing and logging have not yet been measured on healthy live three-source flow.

Two limitations remain explicit. Target-cost RFQs have price-dependent quantity and require reconciliation with exchange sizing semantics; they retain source-disagreement context but receive `target_cost_requires_candidate_dependent_quantity` without candidate prices. Also, the current retained NFL feed configuration still has reviewed-mapping/fee/payoff gates that prevent healthy three-source pricing. Logging those blocked requests demonstrates capture coverage, not executable opportunity. No live feed calls, orders or live risk/config changes were made to install this infrastructure.

Tests cover unchanged baseline quotes/reservations, point alternatives when stressed prices are infeasible, source-sign complementation and real depth, mapping/creator/imputed-depth gates, target-cost exclusion, risk rejection without new reservations, future policy rejection, independent sport profiles and rejection of any attempt to enable orders.
