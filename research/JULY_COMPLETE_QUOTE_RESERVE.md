July 31 does not change the main reserve finding. Point-fair selection and the stressed shadow quote rule evaluate materially different opportunity sets. The full stress bound excludes nearly all point opportunities. The intermediate marginal-upper scenario assumes independent legs and conservative individual probabilities; it is an alternative model assumption, not the original worst-case dependence guarantee.

These results do not prove that charging more causes losses: changing the hurdle changes which customers/trades remain. Nor does the PM point-selected profit establish calibrated three-source probabilities or justify removing safeguards. Pinnacle remains absent. The next model comparison must explicitly calibrate marginal uncertainty and dependence, and evaluate proposed quotes, exposure and fixture concentration on identical inputs.

The completed 14-date component records and July 31 records were combined only after verifying disjoint input paths and all underlying hashes. Additive reserve numerators/quantities were pooled before recomputing weighted averages. Every model's point count and P&L independently reconcile to all 15 daily unlimited summaries. No daily funded P&L was added. Original intermediate outputs remain retained.

A separate continuous 48-policy July-only snapshot has now been launched. Its projected inputs contain only July development dates; source replay hashes were rechecked and July 13's gap preserved. This is the existing margin-grid/accounting evaluation, not yet a new joint-pricing model comparison. Results remain pending until snapshot completion and verification.
