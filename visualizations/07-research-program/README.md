# Volume, markouts, hedging, and inventory research

Open [the self-contained chart atlas](index.html) in a browser. No server, credentials, or external market-data connection is required. Expand a chart's exact-values disclosure to inspect values and sample counts.

Read the [individual findings and methodology](../../research/RESEARCH_FINDINGS_20260910.md) alongside the charts. The atlas contains 20 plots covering six studies; full tables, coverage flags, forecasts, and historical fill ledgers are in `runs/research_program_20260910/`.

These are historical research results, not deployed pricing rules. The later-period splits are chronological replications, not untouched holdouts. Missing depth, uncertain historical fees, dependent games, and hypothetical participation remain explicit limitations.

The project template is `research-atlas.template.html`. Rebuild with the offline pipeline documented in the findings. The template is not a standalone report until populated by the builder.
